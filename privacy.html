隐私政策Privacy Policy

Puppy Glow（小柴补光相机）· 生效日期 2026 年 11 月 9 日 · 适用于 1.0 及以后版本 Puppy Glow · Effective 9 November 2026 · Applies to version 1.0 and later

一句话总结：我们不做账号、不收集个人信息、不上传你的照片和视频。补光、美颜、滤镜全部在你手机上完成，App 在飞行模式下也能完整使用。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

不收集任何个人信息。具体来说：

  • 无需注册或登录，不要求姓名、手机号、邮箱、地址、位置、通讯录。
  • 不读取设备标识符（IDFA / IDFV），不做跨 App 或跨网站追踪，不启用「允许 App 请求跟踪」。
  • 不含广告、统计、崩溃上报、推送服务等任何第三方 SDK。
  • 我们没有服务器，也没有用户数据库——没有可以泄露的东西。

2. 相机、麦克风与相册

  • 相机：只在你打开取景或拍照/录像时使用。画面、人脸检测、美颜与滤镜全部在设备内通过系统框架实时处理，不会上传。
  • 麦克风：在两种情况下使用——录制带声音的视频时；开启「呼吸」灯效时，App 实时读取环境音量让光跟着声音变化。灯效只在本机实时计算音量，不录音、不保存、不上传。
  • 相册（写入）：拍摄完成后，照片或视频会自动存入你的系统相册。
  • 相册（读取）：只用于查看和管理本 App 拍摄的内容。
  • 你可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 小柴补光相机」中关闭任一权限。关闭后仅对应功能不可用，其他功能不受影响。

3. 保存在你手机上的数据

  • 保存内容：自定义色卡与配色库、补光形状、提词器台词、界面偏好，以及免费拍摄次数的计数（用于判断新用户 100 次与之后每月 15 次的免费额度是否用完；只记次数，不含照片、视频或任何个人信息）。购买由 Apple 处理，App 只在本机读取 StoreKit 返回的权益状态。
  • 保存位置：仅保存在你的设备上，不离开设备、不上传。拍摄次数的计数也只在本机计算和保存。这些数据我们收不到，因此不构成数据收集，与「不收集任何数据」的结论一致。
  • 我们能否读取：不能。我们没有任何通道访问这些内容。
  • 如何删除：删除 App 会清除其他本地数据；免费拍摄次数的计数保存在本机钥匙串中，删除 App 后仍保留，它只存在你的手机上，不上传、不同步。系统备份（iCloud / 电脑备份）是否包含这些数据，取决于你的备份设置，由 Apple 处理。

4. 购买与订阅

  • Pro 购买通过 Apple App Store 内购完成，支付与收据由 Apple 处理。
  • 我们收不到你的姓名、地址、支付方式或卡号；App 只在本机向 App Store 查询你是否已开通 Pro（含「恢复购买」）。
  • Apple 按其隐私政策处理交易数据。
  • 管理或取消订阅：iPhone「设置 › Apple 账户 › 订阅」。

5. 分享码

App 可以把一套配色导出成一串分享码，由你自行决定发给谁。分享码只包含配色参数，不含任何个人信息、设备标识或拍摄内容；它不经过我们的服务器（我们没有服务器），我们也看不到你分享给了谁。

6. 儿童隐私

本 App 不收集任何人的信息，因此也不会收集儿童的信息。App 内没有广告，也没有指向外部内容社区的链接。家长可以放心让孩子使用补光与拍照功能。

7. 你的权利

由于我们不持有你的个人数据，访问、更正、删除、导出等请求都可以在你自己的设备上直接完成——删除 App 即可清除 App 内保存的其他本地数据（免费拍摄次数的计数保存在本机钥匙串中，删除 App 后仍保留，见第 3 节）。我们既没有数据可以提供，也没有数据可能被泄露。如仍有疑问，欢迎随时联系我们。

8. 第三方服务

本 App 唯一涉及的外部服务是 Apple 的 App Store（用于内购与订阅状态查询）。除此之外不接入任何第三方服务、不嵌入网页、不发送任何请求到我们或他人的服务器。

9. 政策更新

如果将来新增需要联网或涉及数据的 功能，我们会先更新本页并在 App 内明确提示，之后才会启用。本政策的任何变更都会在此页面公布，并同步更新顶部的生效日期。

10. 联系我们

隐私相关问题请联系：lwjtmj@gmail.com。我们通常在 3 个工作日内回复。

In one sentence: no account, no personal data collected, no photos or videos uploaded. Lighting, beauty and filters all run on your iPhone, and the app works fully in Airplane Mode.

1. What we collect

No personal information. Specifically:

  • No sign-up or login. We never ask for your name, phone number, email, address, location or contacts.
  • We do not read device identifiers (IDFA / IDFV), do not track you across apps or websites, and do not use App Tracking Transparency.
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind — no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no push providers.
  • We operate no servers and keep no user database, so there is nothing that could leak.

2. Camera, microphone and photo library

  • Camera: used only while you frame a shot or record. Frames, face detection, beauty and filters are processed on-device in real time and are never uploaded.
  • Microphone: used in two cases — when you record video with sound, and when the Breathing light effect is on, where the app reads the live sound level so the light follows the sound. The effect only measures volume on your device in real time; nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Photo library (write): after each capture, the photo or video is saved to your system photo library automatically.
  • Photo library (read): used only to browse and manage media captured by this app.
  • You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings → Puppy Glow. Only the matching feature stops working; everything else is unaffected.

3. Data stored on your device

  • What is stored: your custom colour cards and palettes, light shapes, teleprompter scripts, interface preferences, and a count of free captures (used to tell whether the 100 free captures for new users and the 15 free captures per month after that have been used up; it holds only a number — no photos, videos or personal information). Purchases are handled by Apple; the app only reads, on your device, the entitlement status returned by StoreKit.
  • Where: on your device only. It never leaves your device and is never uploaded; the capture count is likewise calculated and stored only on your device. We never receive any of this, so it is not data collection — consistent with our statement that no data is collected.
  • Can we read it: no. We have no channel to access it.
  • Deleting it: deleting the app clears all other local data. The free-capture count is kept in your device's Keychain and remains after the app is deleted; it exists only on your phone and is never uploaded or synced. Whether your device backups (iCloud or computer) include it depends on your backup settings and is handled by Apple.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

  • Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Payment and receipts are handled by Apple.
  • We never receive your name, address, payment method or card number. The app only asks the App Store on your device whether Pro is active, including for Restore Purchases.
  • Apple processes transaction data under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • To manage or cancel: iPhone Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

5. Share codes

The app can export a palette as a share code that you choose to send to whomever you like. A share code contains colour parameters only — no personal data, no device identifiers, no captured media. It does not pass through our servers (we have none) and we cannot see who you shared it with.

6. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children either. There are no ads and no links to external content communities. Parents can let children use the lighting and camera features without concern.

7. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data, access, correction, deletion and portability requests can be satisfied directly on your device — deleting the app clears the other data stored in it (the free-capture count stays in your device's Keychain after deletion; see section 3). We have no data to hand over and none that could be exposed. If you still have questions, just email us.

8. Third parties

The only external service involved is Apple's App Store (in-app purchase and subscription status). We embed no third-party services, no web views, and send no requests to our own or anyone else's servers.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future feature needs network access or involves data, we will update this page and tell you inside the app before enabling it. Any change will be published here and the effective date above will be updated.

10. Contact

For privacy questions: lwjtmj@gmail.com. We usually reply within 3 business days.
