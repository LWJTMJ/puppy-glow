常见问题

App 是做什么的？What does the app do?

把手机屏幕变成一盏可调的补光灯：8 组预设色卡 + 全彩自调 + 亮度无级调节，自带取景小窗、美颜相机、滤镜、实况照片与录像，还有提词器和配色分享码。自拍、拍宝贝、录口播都适用。

It turns your iPhone screen into an adjustable fill light: 8 preset colour cards plus full-colour custom mixing with stepless brightness, a framing preview, a beauty camera, filters, Live Photos and video recording, a teleprompter and shareable colour palettes. Great for selfies, kids' photos and talking-head videos.

为什么照片里没有我？怎么开相机？Why is there no camera view? How do I enable it?

相机页点「开相机」即可。第一次会请求相机权限，请选「允许」。如果之前点了「不允许」：打开 iOS「设置 → 小柴补光相机 → 相机」重新打开。只用屏幕补光（照镜子、给别人打光）则完全不需要相机。

Tap "Open Camera" on the camera page and allow camera access when asked. If you previously denied it, go to iOS Settings → Puppy Glow → Camera and switch it back on. Using the screen purely as a light never requires camera access.

拍好的照片 / 视频去哪了？Where do my photos and videos go?

拍摄完成后，照片或视频会自动存入你的系统相册，打开「照片」App 就能看到。如果没有存进去，检查 iOS「设置 → 小柴补光相机 → 照片」是否为「添加照片」或「所有照片」。所有内容都保存在你自己的相册里，我们不上传任何东西。

After each capture, the photo or video is saved to your system photo library automatically — open the Photos app to find it. If it doesn't appear, check iOS Settings → Puppy Glow → Photos and allow Add Photos or All Photos. Everything stays in your own library; nothing is uploaded.

录像没有声音？My video has no sound.

录音是可选的：切到录像模式后，快门旁边会出现麦克风开关，打开它再录。第一次使用会请求麦克风权限。如果之前拒绝过，去 iOS「设置 → 小柴补光相机 → 麦克风」打开。另外，「呼吸」灯效也会用麦克风实时读取环境音量，让光跟着声音变化；它只在本机计算音量，不录音、不保存、不上传。

Audio recording is optional: in video mode a microphone toggle appears next to the shutter — turn it on before recording. Grant microphone access when first asked, or enable it later in iOS Settings → Puppy Glow → Microphone. The Breathing light effect also uses the microphone to read the live sound level so the light follows the sound; it only measures volume on your device, and nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.

补光太亮 / 太暗，能调吗？Can I adjust brightness?

可以。色卡页有「光强」滑杆无级调节；调色页还能自调色相、饱和度、明度，并保存成自己的色卡。补光时 App 会把屏幕亮度调高；默认退出 App 时自动还原原来的亮度，也可以在设置里关闭「退出时还原亮度」，或随时点「还原屏幕亮度」。

Yes — the colour-card page has a brightness slider with stepless control, and the colour page lets you mix hue, saturation and brightness and save your own cards. While lighting, the app raises the screen brightness; by default your original brightness is restored when you leave the app. You can turn off Restore on Exit in Settings, or restore it any time from Settings.

Pro 会员多少钱？包含什么？What does Pro include and what does it cost?

有两种方式：连续包年含 Apple 官方的 3 天免费试用（新用户首期优惠），试用结束后按年自动续订、由 Apple ID 扣费，试用结束前至少 24 小时在 iPhone「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」中取消就不会扣费；永久买断是一次性付费，没有试用期。参考价：连续包年中国区 ¥1/年、美国区 $0.99/年，永久买断 ¥6 / $2.99，以 App 内和 App Store 显示为准。Pro 包含全部功能：单色/双色/多色补光与动效、形状眼神光、美颜相机与滤镜、实况与录像、提词器、配色库与分享码，终身免费升级。

Two options: the yearly plan includes Apple's official 3-day free trial for new subscribers; after the trial it renews yearly and is charged to your Apple ID. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions and you won't be charged. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment with no trial. Reference prices in the U.S.: $0.99/year or $2.99 lifetime; prices shown in the app and on the App Store apply. Pro unlocks everything: single/two/multi-colour lighting and effects, catchlight shapes, the beauty camera, filters, Live Photos and video, the teleprompter, palette library and share codes — with free lifetime upgrades.

怎么取消订阅或退款？How do I cancel or request a refund?

订阅由 Apple 管理：打开 App Store → 右上角头像 → 订阅，即可查看、更换或取消。取消后可用到当前计费周期结束。退款请联系 Apple（Report a Problem 或联系 Apple 支持），我们无法代为操作。永久买断不产生续费，无需取消。

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: App Store → your avatar → Subscriptions to review, switch or cancel. Access continues until the current period ends. For refunds, contact Apple via Report a Problem or Apple Support — we cannot issue refunds ourselves. The lifetime purchase never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

换了新手机，怎么恢复 Pro？How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

在会员页点「恢复购买」，并使用同一 Apple ID 登录 App Store。购买记录绑定 Apple ID，换机、重装都不会丢。如果恢复失败，确认两点：登录的是当初购买的 Apple ID；设置里没有开启屏幕使用时间限制内购。

Tap Restore Purchases on the membership page while signed in to the same Apple ID. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and survive device changes and reinstalls. If restore fails, check that you are signed in with the purchasing Apple ID and that Screen Time is not blocking in-app purchases.

会收集我的数据吗？需要联网吗？Do you collect data? Does it need internet?

不收集。无账号、无广告、无统计 SDK，拍摄内容与设置只存在本机，删除 App 即清除。补光、拍摄、提词等核心功能完全离线可用；只有查购买状态时才会联系 Apple。详见隐私政策。

No. No account, no ads, no analytics SDKs. Captures and settings stay on-device and are removed when you delete the app. Lighting, capture and the teleprompter work fully offline; only purchase checks talk to Apple. See the privacy policy.
