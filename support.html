常见问题

App 是做什么的？What does the app do?

把手机屏幕变成一盏可调的补光灯：8 组预设色卡 + 全彩自调 + 亮度无级调节，自带取景小窗、美颜相机、滤镜、实况照片与录像，还有提词器和配色分享码。自拍、拍宝贝、录口播都适用。

It turns your iPhone screen into an adjustable fill light: 8 preset colour cards plus full-colour custom mixing with stepless brightness, a framing preview, a beauty camera, filters, Live Photos and video recording, a teleprompter and shareable colour palettes. Great for selfies, kids' photos and talking-head videos.

为什么照片里没有我？怎么开相机？Why is there no camera view? How do I enable it?

相机页点「开相机」即可。第一次会请求相机权限，请选「允许」。如果之前点了「不允许」：打开 iOS「设置 → 小柴补光相机 → 相机」重新打开。只用屏幕补光（照镜子、给别人打光）则完全不需要相机。

Tap "Open Camera" on the camera page and allow camera access when asked. If you previously denied it, go to iOS Settings → Puppy Glow → Camera and switch it back on. Using the screen purely as a light never requires camera access.

拍好的照片 / 视频去哪了？Where do my photos and videos go?

拍完先显示在 App 内，长按图片（视频下方有按钮）选「存储到照片」，才会进入系统相册。如果保存失败，检查 iOS「设置 → 小柴补光相机 → 照片」是否为「添加照片」或「所有照片」。所有内容都保存在你自己的相册里，我们不上传任何东西。

Captures appear inside the app first. Touch and hold a photo (or use the button under a video) and choose Save to Photos to put it in your library. If saving fails, check iOS Settings → Puppy Glow → Photos and allow Add Photos or All Photos. Everything stays in your own library; nothing is uploaded.

录像没有声音？My video has no sound.

录音是可选的：切到录像模式后，快门旁边会出现麦克风开关，打开它再录。第一次使用会请求麦克风权限。如果之前拒绝过，去 iOS「设置 → 小柴补光相机 → 麦克风」打开。

Audio recording is optional: in video mode a microphone toggle appears next to the shutter — turn it on before recording. Grant microphone access when first asked, or enable it later in iOS Settings → Puppy Glow → Microphone.

补光太亮 / 太暗，能调吗？Can I adjust brightness?

可以。色卡页有「光强」滑杆无级调节；调色页还能自调色相、饱和度、明度，并保存成自己的色卡。本 App 不改动系统屏幕亮度，退出 App 后屏幕会恢复原状，不会刺眼。

Yes — the colour-card page has a brightness slider with stepless control, and the colour page lets you mix hue, saturation and brightness and save your own cards. The app never changes the system brightness level, so your screen returns to normal on exit.

Pro 会员多少钱？包含什么？What does Pro include and what does it cost?

所有新用户有 3 天免费试用。之后可选连续包年（中国区 ¥1/年，美国区 $0.99/年）或永久买断（¥6 / $2.99，不自动续费）。Pro 包含全部功能：单色/双色/多色补光与动效、形状眼神光、美颜相机与滤镜、实况与录像、提词器、配色库与分享码，终身免费升级。

Every new user gets a 3-day free trial. Afterwards choose a yearly auto-renewing plan ($0.99/year in the U.S.) or a one-time lifetime purchase ($2.99, no renewal). Pro unlocks everything: single/two/multi-colour lighting and effects, catchlight shapes, the beauty camera, filters, Live Photos and video, the teleprompter, palette library and share codes — with free lifetime upgrades.

怎么取消订阅或退款？How do I cancel or request a refund?

订阅由 Apple 管理：打开 App Store → 右上角头像 → 订阅，即可查看、更换或取消。取消后可用到当前计费周期结束。退款请联系 Apple（Report a Problem 或联系 Apple 支持），我们无法代为操作。永久买断不产生续费，无需取消。

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: App Store → your avatar → Subscriptions to review, switch or cancel. Access continues until the current period ends. For refunds, contact Apple via Report a Problem or Apple Support — we cannot issue refunds ourselves. The lifetime purchase never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

换了新手机，怎么恢复 Pro？How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

在会员页点「恢复购买」，并使用同一 Apple ID 登录 App Store。购买记录绑定 Apple ID，换机、重装都不会丢。如果恢复失败，确认两点：登录的是当初购买的 Apple ID；设置里没有开启屏幕使用时间限制内购。

Tap Restore Purchases on the membership page while signed in to the same Apple ID. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID and survive device changes and reinstalls. If restore fails, check that you are signed in with the purchasing Apple ID and that Screen Time is not blocking in-app purchases.

会收集我的数据吗？需要联网吗？Do you collect data? Does it need internet?

不收集。无账号、无广告、无统计 SDK，拍摄内容与设置只存在本机，删除 App 即清除。补光、拍摄、提词等核心功能完全离线可用；只有查购买状态时才会联系 Apple。详见隐私政策。

No. No account, no ads, no analytics SDKs. Captures and settings stay on-device and are removed when you delete the app. Lighting, capture and the teleprompter work fully offline; only purchase checks talk to Apple. See the privacy policy.
